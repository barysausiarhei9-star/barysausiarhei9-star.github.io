THE CREATOR’S LOT

Elena Berezina

(as systems theory requires)

1. The creator of the system "ALL THAT EXISTS" cannot be OUTSIDE the system, because if he is not part of the system defined as "All That Exists", then he simply DOES NOT EXIST. 2. Nor can he be part of the system "ALL THAT EXISTS" alongside his own creation, because a part is always simpler than the whole, and the creator cannot be simpler than the system he has created. 3. Only one way remains: to identify the Creator with the Creation:

THE CREATOR IS ALL THAT EXISTS.

Russian original: Samlib.
