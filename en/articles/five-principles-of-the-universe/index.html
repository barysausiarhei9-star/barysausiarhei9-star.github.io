FIVE PRINCIPLES OF THE UNIVERSE

PRINCIPLE ONE

The law of conservation, affirming the eternity, indestructibility and interconversion of matter and energy, encompasses all physical categories without exception: matter, antimatter and energy of all kinds and forms, including entropy as part of the positive energy of the system and negentropy, i.e. information, which, according to Shannon, is entropy with the opposite sign.

PRINCIPLE TWO

Cosmic objects consisting of antimatter are in principle invisible to us because, having a speed greater than that of light, they are always in the reverse light cone relative to the observer (in negative time), but their existence is confirmed by indirect evidence — their undeniable influence on the bodies of the material Universe, which is what makes astrophysicists search for the notorious "dark matter".

PRINCIPLE THREE

The Big Bang is nothing other than the annihilation of all the matter (mass) existing in the world with all the antimatter contained in it. From the energy of the explosion, the development of antipodal worlds begins again, until their next annihilation. This process is cyclical and eternal, like that for a "particle-antiparticle" pair.

PRINCIPLE FOUR

Whether a quantum-mechanical object is a particle or a wave depends exclusively on the speed of that object: at subluminal speeds it is a particle; at the speed of light or at speeds close to it, it is a wave. Upon reaching a superluminal speed, the object manifests itself as an antiparticle — with corresponding changes in all other parameters.

PRINCIPLE FIVE

Time, like spatial dimensions, has two directions along the coordinate axis — in both directions from zero—that is, from the flash (“event”): positive time in the forward light cone and negative time in the reverse one. The movement of any object towards the observer is always the positive direction of movement, and away from the observer is the negative direction, both in space and in time.
