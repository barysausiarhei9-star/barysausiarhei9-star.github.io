ON ALL KINDS OF “MYSTERIES”

Elena Berezina

It is strange to hear so much talk about the “mystery of the origin of the psyche”, the “mystery of consciousness”, and the like. For a consistent evolutionist, there is nothing “mysterious” about these phenomena. Nor is there for anyone familiar with the fundamentals of cybernetics.

W. Ross Ashby observed: “Every open dynamic system, provided that the physical laws operating within it remain constant for a sufficiently long time, produces its own form of intelligent life.”

Of course, this statement should not be understood as confirmation that “little green men” exist somewhere in the cosmos. To be surprised that “there is no life on Mars” while life exists on Earth is rather like being surprised that fingers do not grow out of our ears even though they grow on our hands. The point is simply that the emergence of life in general, and of thinking beings in particular, is a natural process.

It remains only to add that this process occurs as living organisms evolve the ability to detect, harness, and put to their own use the available “cosmic substrates”: first, for example, electromagnetic radiation (the use of sunlight in photosynthesis), and later information (negentropy), through the development of s i g n a l i n g activity, or the higher nervous activity of living systems.
