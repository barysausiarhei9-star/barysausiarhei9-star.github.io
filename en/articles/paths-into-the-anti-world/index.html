Paths into the Anti-World Have Yet to Be Trodden

Elena Berezina

Entropy is PART of a system’s energy. The part that cannot be converted into work. The dissipation of energy leads to an increase in the system’s entropy. As part of the positive energy of the universe, entropy is naturally positive.

As for information, or negentropy… Boltzmann established the relationship between entropy and probability. In 1948, Wiener stated that the amount of information, being the NEGATIVE LOGARITHM of a quantity that can be regarded as a probability, is essentially a kind of NEGATIVE ENTROPY.

What I emphasize is this: yes, negentropy, NEGATIVE entropy, or information, is part of energy that is itself negative, as observed in the anti-world, where all physical categories change sign.

From science news: “Geneva physicists regard antihydrogen atoms as a ticket … to ANOTHER MATHEMATICAL UNIVERSE, where ‘everything positive becomes negative, and left becomes right’.”

More precisely, a ticket to another physical reality, described by different mathematics, where, upon crossing into the “looking-glass world” (CPT symmetry), the POSITIVE becomes negative: space, its curvature, time (which is inseparable from space, since cosmic time is measured by distance), mass (or, alternatively, the existing square of an imaginary quantity, on both sides of the observer along a single line of sight—which would yield minus one as a real value), and energy in all its constituent parts, including entropy, which changes sign because time running backward makes entropic processes reversible.

And once again, there is nothing heretical about this:

Perpetual-motion machines do not and cannot exist… in half of the system, in the material world. But the totality of ANTIPODES—the structural principle of the COMPLETE (contradictory) system—is precisely the only possible Perpetual-Motion Machine: the ABSOLUTE.

That is, the indestructibility and eternity of the world are due to nothing other than its duality. The Law of Antipodes. Like particles, worlds arise only in pairs and disappear in pairs as well: direct and reverse annihilation of two exact antipodal counterparts. Only annihilation provides a path to the COMPLETE conversion of matter into energy, into radiation, and vice versa.

I wish to emphasize that I regard INFORMATION as a physical category in its own right, the antipode of the universe’s entropy. Its existence does not require an “observer” at all. Cosmic fields of information are an objective reality. Moreover, since waves (massless particles, quanta) can serve as their “own” antipodes, entropy itself can also be called information: a measure of chaos is the same as a measure of orderliness, but with the opposite sign.

Thus, the NEGATIVE LOGARITHM, used instead of the positive one employed in entropy calculations, is the mathematical principle for calculating negentropy.

In general, the principle of antipodes is “easy to use”: do not ignore negative and imaginary numbers, which, in fact, constantly arise in cosmological calculations, remembering that “Algebra is wiser than the natural scientist.”

A Devil’s Dozen of Devilishly Interesting Thoughts

  1. The antipode of a particle is an antiparticle.
  2. A particle and a wave are two names for the same essence.
  3. The antipode of a wave is an anti-wave.
  4. Energy-carrying (massless) particles (waves) are their own antipodes.
  5. A quantum of energy is its own antipode.
  6. Entropy is a form of energy.
  7. The antipode of entropy is called negentropy.
  8. Negentropy is synonymous with information.
  9. Entropy can also be regarded as information, since it is its “own” antipode.
  10. Entropy is a measure of a system’s chaos and uncertainty.
  11. Information is a measure of orderliness.
  12. In measuring chaos, we measure orderliness, but with a negative sign.

If it is still unclear what this means, here is a hint: both categories, entropy and negentropy, are information—its dual (plus one/minus one) code. Note: as mentioned above, minus one can be the square of an imaginary quantity.
