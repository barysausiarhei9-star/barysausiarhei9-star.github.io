Meow

Elena Berezina

I have done my part — I have discovered a new physical reality, one that requires a new mathematical framework. Incidentally, I have proposed the final formula myself: it is simple, as a “Formula of Everything” ought to be. It is simple because it consists of a combination of mathematical symbols representing two complete opposites, capable of annihilating each other: Universe = E = mc2 Anti-Universe = -E = - mc2 = (-1)E = (-1)mc2 considering that -1 = i2 (the imaginary unit squared), Anti-Universe = (i2)E = (i2)mc2 Then UNIVERSE + ANTI-UNIVERSE = E + (-E) = mc2 + (- mc2) = 0 = NOTHING or: E+ (i2)E = mc2 + (i2)mc2 = EVERYTHING (a specific quantity expressed as a complex number) In fact, instead of the words “Universe” and “Anti-Universe”, one could use “Particle” and “Antiparticle” (or, for that matter, any pair of opposites with any values of E and m). I assert a COMPLETE ANALOGY between the only ETERNAL system known to modern science — a particle-antiparticle pair periodically appearing and disappearing in the vacuum — and... universes that are opposites of one another, which, like particles, are born and disappear in the cosmos in the process of an ANNIHILATIONAL Big Bang. Like particles — from the energy of annihilation. Like particles — cyclically and eternally. Frankly, I find it difficult to understand why almost all educated people, with rare exceptions, rise up in protest upon hearing, for example, about negative energy. Even gravitation is entirely negative energy, although it is measured by the positive energy expended in overcoming it. Hawking, in particular, does not even doubt that when a particle-antiparticle pair emerges in a vacuum, the energy of the latter is negative, simply by virtue of the conservation law, which does not allow energy to arise from nothing. Only the emergence of opposites that together produce “zero increase” is permissible. But mass and energy are equivalent; that is, the mass of an antiparticle must necessarily also be negative (remember Dirac). I am prepared to concede that this is so only at the moment of annihilation, of mutual destruction with a particle (quantities that mutually cancel cannot, from a purely mathematical point of view, fail to have opposite signs). In negative time, however, mass is more likely imaginary: If a particle is in motion, E = m0 c2 , where m0 - rest mass = ..... the formula becomes distorted in transmission, so I shall clarify it in words: gamma equals one divided by the square root of... the expression under the root is: one minus “v” squared divided by “c” squared. For particles moving at subluminal speed, gamma is positive and energy is positive. If the speed exceeds the speed of light, gamma is imaginary. Then, with real (albeit negative) energy, the rest mass will be imaginary. The same applies to “distorted” time, since velocity enters as a square. Such hypothetical particles have been called tachyons. I would regard them simply as a state of an antiparticle. “But if they did not exist, they would be worth inventing.” :) So, the mass is imaginary. The momentum of such a particle equals Now I shall try to explain briefly what I think about entropy. Entropy is a PART of a system's energy. That part of it which cannot be converted into work. (Energy dissipation leads to an increase in the entropy of a system.) As part of the universe's positive energy, it (entropy) is naturally positive. As for information, or negentropy... Boltzmann established the connection between entropy and probability. In 1948, Wiener stated that the quantity of information, being the NEGATIVE LOGARITHM of a quantity that can be regarded as a probability, is essentially a certain NEGATIVE ENTROPY. Which is precisely what I confirm: yes, negentropy, NEGATIVE entropy, information — is part of negative energy, which is observed in the antiworld, where all physical categories change sign. I have quoted the following before, but I shall repeat it: From science news: “Geneva physicists consider antihydrogen atoms a ‘ticket’ ... to ANOTHER MATHEMATICAL UNIVERSE, where ‘positive becomes negative, and left becomes right’.” More precisely, it is a ticket to another physical reality described by different mathematics, where, upon crossing into the “looking-glass world” (CPT symmetry), everything POSITIVE becomes negative: space, its curvature, time (which is inseparable from space, since cosmic time is measured by distance), mass (the square of the imaginary quantity — one in each direction from the observer along the same line of sight — will yield minus one in the real domain), energy — in all its constituent parts, including entropy, which changes sign because time flowing backward causes entropy processes to become reversible). And once again, there is nothing heretical about this: Perpetual-motion machines do not and cannot exist... in one half of the system, in the material world. But a combination of OPPOSITES — the principle underlying the structure of a COMPLETE (contradictory) system — is precisely the only possible Perpetual-Motion Machine: the ABSOLUTE. That is, the indestructibility and eternity of the world are due to nothing other than its duality. The Law of Opposites. :)) Like particles, worlds arise only in pairs and disappear in pairs: direct and reverse annihilation of two exact counterparts that are opposites. Only annihilation provides the path for the COMPLETE conversion of matter into energy, radiation, and vice versa. I want to emphasize that I regard INFORMATION as a fully fledged physical category, the opposite of universal entropy. Its existence does not require the presence of an “observer”. Cosmic fields of information are an objective reality. Moreover, since waves (massless particles, quanta) can be opposites “to themselves”, entropy itself can also be called information: a measure of chaos is the same as a measure of order, but with the opposite sign. Thus, the NEGATIVE LOGARITHM, used instead of the positive one when calculating entropy, is the mathematical principle for calculating negentropy (which, among other things, was something you were interested in). In general, the principle of opposites is “easy to use”: do not ignore negative and imaginary numbers, which, as a matter of fact, constantly arise in cosmological calculations: “algebra is smarter than the natural scientist”, as N. Maksimov said. And it is LOGIC, rather than experiment, that should be placed at the forefront when dealing with cosmological problems. In our world, as I have already said, there are no genuine antiparticles: our world is a world of positive time. When obtaining antiparticles for examination, we... extract them from negative time. And the experiment influences its own result: CPT symmetry is violated, and the antiparticle CHANGES its properties, in particular displaying positive mass — in violation of the conservation law, which in itself is not surprising, but must be properly understood. Nothing other than “crossing the boundary” brings about a change in parameters, which are more important than the particle's “name”. Once again: Objects made of antimatter obey the rule of CPT symmetry. One of its requirements states that the time in which such an object exists must flow backward, must be negative. Antimatter lives in negative time (see “God's Clocks”). Furthermore, only objects moving at SUPERLUMINAL speed can exist in negative time. Recall the classic little children's rhyme “on the subject”: “Today at noon a rocket took to flight, It flies MUCH FASTER THAN THE SPEED OF LIGHT, And reaches its destination at seven in the morning — yesterday.” Ordinary positive mass, as is well known, cannot attain not only superluminal speed but even the speed of light. Therefore, this mass is NOT “ordinary positive” mass. THE PHYSICAL CATEGORIES OF THE ANTIWORLD CHANGE SIGN. Well, in conclusion, one more “self-quotation”. It seems to me that the following points are worth reflecting upon: 1. The opposite of a particle is an antiparticle. 2. Particle and wave are two names for the same essence. 3. The opposite of a wave is an antiwave. 4. Energy-carrying (massless) particles (waves) are opposites to themselves. 5. A quantum of energy is its own opposite. 6. Entropy is a form of energy. 7. The opposite of entropy is called negentropy. 8. Negentropy is synonymous with information. 9. Entropy can also be considered information, since it is its “own opposite”. 10. Entropy is a measure of chaos, of the uncertainty of a system. 12. Information is a measure of order. 13. By measuring chaos, we measure order, but with a negative sign. I think I shall stop here.
