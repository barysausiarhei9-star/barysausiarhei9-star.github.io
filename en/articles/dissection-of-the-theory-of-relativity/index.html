DISSECTION OF THE THEORY OF RELATIVITY

Elena Berezina

The principle of the relativity of time is the possibility of its negative increase. The degree and speed at which an object moves away from an observer are factors that influence the magnitude of this change, up to the transition into negative time itself at superluminal speeds, the compression of time at subluminal speeds, or the nullification of time, accompanied by the transformation of the object into a wave (its multiplication) at the speed of light.
