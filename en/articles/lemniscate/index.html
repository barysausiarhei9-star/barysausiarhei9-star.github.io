The Lemniscate

Elena Berezina

Acknowledging the existence of a CPT-symmetric Anti-World makes it possible to:

  • dispense with the need for any external, supernatural force (a Creator) to bring the Universe into being.
  • find an explanation for how the Universe can arise from nothing without violating physical laws.
  • show the possibility of an eternal world.
  • understand where the energy of the Big Bang came from.
  • explain the nature of gravity.
  • justify a preference for Friedmann’s first model (until now, all three have been regarded as equally probable).
  • explain the emergence of an apparent “excess of particles” over antiparticles in the primordial Universe.
  • establish the cause of the curvature of space and time postulated by Einstein.
  • justify the use of imaginary (and complex) numbers in cosmological calculations.
  • complete the time scale so that time, like the other—spatial—coordinates, has two directions, extending both ways from zero.
  • resolve the paradox of irreversibility, whereby microprocesses (quantum-mechanical ones) appear reversible, while macroprocesses do not.
  • explain the dual nature of particles (their wave properties).

And finally, as regards predicting possible outcomes of possible experiments:

  • suggest that, when light is slowed down (the latest achievement of experimental physics), the photon in all likelihood loses its wave properties. (To test this, the double-slit experiment must be repeated in an apparatus that slows down light).

P.S. The much-discussed “Quantum Entanglement” amply confirms the acquisition of wave properties upon reaching relativistic speeds.
