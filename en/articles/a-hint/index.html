A HINT

Elena Berezina

If a photon from the Sun has been traveling for 7 minutes, it is not yet visible to an observer on Earth. It will become visible — in one minute. Therefore, the coordinate of this point is MINUS ONE. Do you understand what I'm getting at? Plus seven or minus one. Coordinates PLUS SEVEN or MINUS ONE. This is precisely what the RELATIVITY OF TIME is. Depending on the observer's location. (And the ninth minute for this event is once again minus one for the observer. Invisible again.)

Continuing my reflections on the category of time: If movement from point A to point B occurs with an increase in magnitude, then movement from B to A must automatically ("by default") be characterized by a decrease in that magnitude.

Russian original: Samlib.
