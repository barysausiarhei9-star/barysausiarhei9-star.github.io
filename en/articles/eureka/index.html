EUREKA

Elena Berezina

God may be called the Genetic Matrix of the World —
Information, a code — double, like the DNA helix.
Yet you have fashioned yourselves an idol out of absurdity...
GOD IS EVERYTHING. This thought is still beyond the grasp of simple minds.

WORLD (GOD) = UNIVERSE + ANTI-UNIVERSE = E−E = mc² − mc² = 1 − 1 = 0 = NOTHING

However: −1 = i², therefore

NATURE (GOD) = 1 + (−1) = 1 + i² = EVERYTHING

That is, a perfectly concrete quantity expressed by a complex number.

This is the formula Einstein and Niels Bohr struggled with long ago in Copenhagen. But they confined themselves to the natural numbers — and that was a mistake.

I am convinced that all existing numbers must be brought into play — imaginary numbers and complex numbers alike. And only then will everything finally fall into place, rather than just a private beer pipeline running straight from the Carlsberg brewery to the Danish Nobel laureate's own home! )))

Russian original: Samlib.
