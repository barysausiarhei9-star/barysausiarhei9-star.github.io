Time Zones of the Universe

Elena Berezina

Cosmic time is determined by the distance that light travels during that time.

 That is, the greater the distance covered by photons, the more time has elapsed.

  We see light later than the emission of photons occurred. The farther away from us that emission took place, the later we see it. Conclusion: all “events” are situated in a different time, in the past relative to the observer’s own time. That is, according to how far the “events” are from the observer, depending on the conventional value of the time coordinates of the observer’s location, there is a decrease in the absolute values of positive quantities denoting time, or an increase in the absolute values of negative time quantities for the observed “event”. Moreover, this principle applies to any observer, wherever they may be.

 This is the foundation, the origin of the theory of relativity. The physical meaning of the relativity of time.

 A natural regularity in the arrangement of the universe’s “time zones”.

Russian original: Samlib.
