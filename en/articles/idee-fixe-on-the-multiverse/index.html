IDÉE FIXE ON THE MULTIVERSE

Elena Berezina

“Real science is done by freaks.” (A. Nevzorov)

God was invented to explain how the world came into being.
The assertion that God created the world raises a question: and who created God? Creationists reply: God is eternal.
Scientists have their doubts: might this mysterious God be a superfluous entity in that case? After all, according to the Law of Conservation, the physical world itself is eternal!
Not everyone is equipped to understand scientific reasoning: people dislike delving into anything complicated or obscure.
The clergy resourcefully declared that faith requires no proof whatsoever. One must believe unconditionally in any nonsense.
The result: to this day there are far more believers on Earth than atheists and agnostics. A stupid idea takes hold of the masses more easily.
But enthusiasts of scientific oddities refuse to give up.
What if, instead of believing unconditionally, we tried to find scientific evidence for God's existence?
What, for example, would cybernetics—that whore of imperialism—tell us?
The physical world undoubtedly exists. Let us suppose that God, the creator of that world, exists as well.
Then CREATOR AND CREATION together form a system that may be designated by the term “ALL THAT EXISTS”. It is ALL-ENCOMPASSING. Whatever lies outside this system does not exist, by definition. Thus, the WORLD and GOD are two constituent parts of a single cybernetic system.
But System Theory tells us that the creator of a system cannot be simpler than the system he has created. And a part is always simpler than the whole. Even if the world subsequently becomes more complex through self-improvement, the creator remains a part of the system “ALL THAT EXISTS”, never sticking his nose outside it lest he “fall out of existence”.
A paradox: he cannot remain outside the system, yet he cannot be merely a part of it.
The solution is obvious: the Creator must be identified with creation: GOD IS ALL THAT EXISTS., that is, GOD IS THE WORLD.
Only in this form, and no other, do we retain any possibility of speaking about a divine essence at all. Why? Good question! But, as the song goes, “is it worth talking about?”.
Where were we? GOD is the WORLD, and it is ETERNAL. How, then, can this Perpetual Motion Machine exist, considering that it is perhaps almost the only possible “perpetual-motion machine” of any kind?
And so we move smoothly on to the question of the prerequisites for eternity.

***

A question arises: is there any known, sufficiently well-studied ETERNAL SYSTEM in the world?
Imagine that such a system exists! It is the virtual “particle/antiparticle” pair, appearing and disappearing in the vacuum through annihilation, owing to fluctuations of the equally eternal physical field.
Everyone knows that matter and energy can transform into one another.
There is no more famous formula in the world than Einstein's equation expressing these transformations. It is hard to find anyone who has never heard that “E equals m c squared”.
The relation describes the mutual transformations of mass and energy, stating that energy is equivalent to mass multiplied by the square of the speed of light. And if energy, God forbid, is negative, then mass will be negative as well, since the speed of light appears squared.
Annihilation is the only means of completely converting matter into radiation. More precisely, matter and antimatter into radiation and back again. For annihilation requires antipodes—quantities of the same kind but bearing opposite signs.
The term “annihilation” (nihil—nothing) is used for the phenomenon in which a particle and its corresponding antiparticle “disappear”, turning into photons or still lighter quanta of an electromagnetic or other field. Pair production is the reverse process, in which the interaction of physical fields simultaneously gives rise to a particle and an antiparticle. It is worth recalling here that field quanta (energy-carrying particles) may be their own antipodes and are therefore capable of annihilation.

Because “the great is repeated in the small”, mutually annihilating antipodes may be utterly “elementary” or infinitely complex: from quarks to universes. The Big Bang (also known as the Big Crunch) is likewise nothing more than ordinary annihilation of matter with antimatter. Only the scale differs.
Naturally, the more complex the structures participating in this endless cyclic process, the longer the interval between annihilations.
In the case of entire worlds (a universe and an anti-universe), that means many billions of years.

***

The total sum of all parameters in each pair of antipodes is always zero, because the physical quantities of each mirrored counterpart have opposite signs.
Geneva physicists regard the study of antimatter as a journey into another mathematical universe, where everything positive becomes negative and left becomes right.
But they ought to say: INTO ANOTHER PHYSICAL REALITY. Into a world where, under the rule of CPT symmetry, space, its curvature, time, and other quantities acquire the minus sign characteristic of the “looking-glass world”.
Mass in the symmetrical reality is represented by the square of tachyonic, imaginary mass. (Taking account of our familiar baryonic mass of the same magnitude, “three quarks for Mr. Mark” begin to emerge.)
But an imaginary number, when squared, yields a negative number; thus, at the moment of the “Big Bang”, the mutually annihilating quantities readily acquire strictly opposite signs. Otherwise, they would add up instead of cancelling out.
Accordingly, energy in the antiworld is negative too, including entropy, since time flowing backwards entails the inevitable reversibility of entropic processes and cause-and-effect relationships. Negative entropy is information, according to Brillouin's definition—an author seldom mentioned in “the broad circles of narrow specialists”.
How can such a dual system be represented mathematically?
That is not difficult, for the combined total of the masses, among other quantities, of complete antipodal counterparts is zero.

***

It has long been known (algebra is cleverer than the natural scientist) that zero is a complex number, containing real and imaginary parts.
The imaginary unit is represented by the symbol “i”; to the first power it is the square root of minus one, and raised to the second power—“I^2” (“i” squared)—it equals minus one.
Therefore, THE FORMULA OF EVERYTHING:

0 = Nothing = x-x = x +(- x) = x + (-1) (x) = x + (i^2)(x) = Something

In the annihilating interaction of worlds, “x” might represent, for example, the total baryonic mass of the universe. One could use either side of Einstein's equation... but the magnitude itself is of no importance, provided we do not forget its exact corresponding antipode.
Yet “SOMETHING”, on the scale of universes, is synonymous with “EVERYTHING”.
Even if there are multiple pairs of such worlds (out of respect for old Occam, we may disregard them), the principle remains unchanged:
what is self-sufficient and eternal is a dual, dialectically contradictory entity.
The foremost prerequisite, the indispensable condition of eternity, is the existence of antipodes.
The physical world is eternal according to the Law of Conservation precisely because, alongside the material Universe, it includes an imaginary (“virtual”) component: an ANTI-UNIVERSE consisting of antimatter.
Why is the idea of an anti-universe, which has occurred to scientists more than once, no longer popular?
It was rejected because even the seemingly most sophisticated optical instruments had long failed to detect its objects. The patience of learned gentlemen finally ran out.
But how did it happen that, even after discovering dark matter, they never returned to the anti-universe hypothesis?
Quantum physicists speak of negative mass, faster-than-light speeds, and the like only in passing, furtively, and with a guilty air.
Discredited and ridiculed, the idea of a CPT-symmetric antiworld still “hangs in the air” and... awaits its hour.

***

“Dark” matter, which cannot be visually detected, is actually an accumulation of supposedly “vanished” antimatter. The antipodes are separated solely by a speed barrier, and the speed of objects existing in negative time is superluminal. “When you fly much faster than light, you'll reach your destination at seven in the morning—yesterday.”
By and large, ALL THAT EXISTS is INFORMATION, with its inherent binary code.
Yes indeed: one and zero. But with zero's complex nature taken into account, allowing the use of imaginary and negative numbers, which are indispensable for describing the metamorphoses of that single fundamental essence whose existence Lee Smolin suspects.
A system claiming completeness is necessarily contradictory, and it is naive to suppose that it can be described solely within, say, the positive integers of the natural number sequence. So when applying renormalization, think of the yellow monkey!
The cybernetic system “ALL THAT EXISTS” is possible, first, in the form of an information field—dual, naturally enough (a measure of chaos is nothing other than a measure of order with the opposite sign).
Second, it may take the form of any other energy field whose quanta act as their own antipodes.
And finally, the energy of annihilation may be embodied in the structural elements of antipodal worlds, which gradually develop and then slowly undergo involution.
The process continues until the next annihilation, and the whole cycle repeats ad infinitum. Information is thus a kind of “genetic matrix of the world”.
And how could one fail to recall the “DNA double helix” of the inconsolable Avery?

***

The most meticulous thinkers have long been puzzling over where all the antiparticles went. The primordial universe teemed with antipodal pairs. Annihilation evidently never took place: the material world is right here before us! What is more, if we make an effort, we can even summon, say, antihydrogen out of nonexistence. “Know-how”!
So the correct answer is: we may not see the ground squirrel, but it is there! Antimatter is hiding from us somewhere.
Get those brains working.
We see only light that reaches the “rods and cones”.
Photons need some time to travel from a light source to the receptors of the retina. Every flash (emission of photons) is recorded later than it occurs. Thus, the movement of photons—and of everything else—from an “event” toward an “observer” takes place from past to future (the normal flow of time).
Movement away from the observer, however, is directed backwards—into the past.
Movement from future to past may occur either through a decrease in the magnitude of positive numbers or through an increase in the magnitude of negative ones, depending on the value of the initial moment in time.
With a negative increment of a positive magnitude, subluminal speed makes it possible to register only the compression or slowing of positive time.
At the speed of light, the negative increment “cancels out” the entire passage of positive time. Time becomes zero; it stops. Energy-carrying particles “spin their wheels” in time and, displaying wave properties, multiply.
Can you guess what happens to superluminal objects? Exactly! Time for anything faster than light always carries a minus sign, which is why such objects are cosmic invisibles.

***

To reject negative time and its extraordinary speeds is tantamount to rejecting the theory of relativity.
Einstein himself bears some responsibility for this predicament, having once declared: “Since all our experience provides no evidence of violations of cause-and-effect relationships, the impossibility of faster-than-light speeds may be considered proven, even though the existence of a mechanism for transmitting a signal in which the effect precedes the cause contains no logical contradictions.”
And only the obstinate Gell-Mann, author of the “law of conservation of strangeness”, muttered gloomily in reply: “Everything that is not forbidden is mandatory!”
Honor and praise to the stubborn man!

author of the law of antipodes,
