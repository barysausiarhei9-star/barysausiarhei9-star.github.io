The Secret of Eternity

Elena Berezina

only a cyclic process is eternal,
one that is inherently reversible.
two antipodes—the manifest and the imaginary—
the world of the Absolute, a wonder without wonders,
where relative time and space
ensure the constancy of all categories.

How can any system be eternal?

The only eternal pair reliably known to science—the “particle/antiparticle” pair—now appears (or manifests itself), now is “zeroed out” in the vacuum, existing alternately as particles and as a field.

Apparently, in principle, only that which, while existing, as it were does not exist at all can be eternal as a whole.

Namely, a pair of antipodal counterparts whose characteristics, taken in pairs, all sum to zero.

Zero, as is known, is a complex number. It contains real and imaginary parts.

Nothing = x - x = 0 = x + i^2(x) = Something

That is, SOMETHING can exist eternally only by being equal to “NOTHING”.

The world, too, is eternal in accordance with the Law of Conservation precisely because, alongside the material Universe, it includes an imaginary component—the ANTI-UNIVERSE, consisting of antimatter.

Only a speed barrier separates them, and the speed of the antipodes located in negative time is superluminal.

All in all, EVERYTHING THAT EXISTS is INFORMATION, with its inherent binary code. Sometimes “in pure form” (entropy/negentropy), sometimes embodied in the structures of antipodal worlds that develop cyclically and undergo involution.

During “materialization”, the negative unit of the code is the square of the imaginary unit. Take note: quark triads have “glimmered into view”.
