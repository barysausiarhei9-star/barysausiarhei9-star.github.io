EPILOGUE

Elena Berezina

The rule of CPT symmetry will help us understand that the symmetrical world is, in fact, the world of antimatter. Besides the real world, there is undoubtedly a symmetrical world (synonyms: mirror, virtual, imaginary, tachyonic, “holographic”) — on either side along the line of sight from the baryonic counterpart. With space curved on relativistic scales — it will resemble “globules” of three conventionally identical units, like quark triads. Imaginary particles create an equally imaginary, virtual, invisible world within the reverse cones of every event in the space-time continuum — an antipodal counterpart of the material (physical) universe. A world of superluminal velocities and perverted cause-and-effect relationships. The Anti-Universe.

Russian original: Samlib.
