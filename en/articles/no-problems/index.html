NO PROBLEMS!

Elena Berezina

"As I have repeatedly emphasized, an experiment means nothing at all until it is interpreted by theory".

Max Born

The American scientist Lee Smolin, author of the theory of loop quantum gravity, speaking of the problems of modern theoretical physics, singles out, among others, three of the most important:

1. The need to unite general relativity and quantum theory into one that could lay claim to being a complete theory of physics.
2. To find out whether the various particles and forces can be explained as manifestations of a single fundamental entity.
3. To determine what "dark matter"("dark energy") is.

All the problems listed are easily solvable if we accept the complete analogy I assert between a pair of antipodal particles eternally existing in the vacuum and a pair of antipodal worlds: universe/anti-universe.

Such an approach reveals the need for a single principle of description (including mathematical description) for these two systems, since the difference between them lies exclusively in scale, but not in the way they function.

The unifying formulation must necessarily take into account the antimatter unobservable in the cosmic macroworld, which, with certain errors of perception, can be observed when working with the micro-objects of quantum mechanics.

Apparently, it is precisely antimatter that constitutes so-called "dark" matter, which, like virtual particles, is not registered by optical instruments, but nevertheless exerts an obvious influence on objects of the material world.

And - undoubtedly, there is some single fundamental entity which, depending on the circumstances (more precisely - speed), manifests itself in three possible modes-"hypostases":

at subluminal speed it is a material particle(or -structure) with ordinary, positive mass,

upon reaching the speed of light(or approaching it) - it is a wave(a massless, "energy-carrying" particle ("clump"),

at a speed exceeding that of light - it is an antiparticle(structure), possessing under certain conditions an "imaginary" mass, the square of which will at some moment in time yield the negative mass necessary for annihilation with its own antipodal copy in material form.

It should be emphasized that antimatter lives in a "cpt-symmetric" world characterized, above all, by negative time. It is precisely in "perverted" space-time, in its reverse cone, that all superluminal cosmic objects are located; they remain invisible because light cannot catch up with them and be reflected by them. We, however, can see exclusively sources of light - generated or reflected, and only at strictly defined moments requiring the distance between the flash and the observer to correspond to the time it takes a photon to cover that distance (for details on negative time - see "The Lord God's Clock").

The annihilation of all the matter of the universe with all antimatter is the Big Bang ("Time Zero"), providing the energy for the subsequent new cycle of formation of antipodal worlds. The very same clear analogy with particles, except that the interval between annihilations is not microseconds, but - billions of years.

And no "unsolvable" problems: "knock, and it shall be opened unto you!"

SOLVING THE "UNSOLVABLE"

1.The Creator of ALL THAT EXISTS must himself belong to the system "ALL THAT EXISTS", for otherwise he DOES NOT EXIST.

2. If he belongs to the system as a part of it, then he is SIMPLER than the system, since a PART is always simpler than the WHOLE.

3.The Creator of a system CANNOT BE SIMPLER than the system he himself created.

4. Only one way out remains: IDENTIFY the Creator with Creation:

GOD IS ALL THAT EXISTS (neither more, nor less).
